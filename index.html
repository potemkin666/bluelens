Search-first workflow

Drop an image, inspect locally, then choose when to upload.

BlueLens works best as a fast launchpad with explicit operator consent: inspect locally first, then choose if you want a temporary public URL for provider review.

  • Choose once to inspect locally; uploads happen only after you ask for launchpad links.
  • Keep hashes, EXIF, OCR, and compare tools one click away.
  • Launch Lens first, then use the prepared manual links for the rest.
